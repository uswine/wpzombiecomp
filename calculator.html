Model: gravity torque Tg(θ)=W·L·cos(θ−β) about a hinge pin just above the door's top edge; ribbon springs give a nearly flat opening torque n·k·(preload−θ/360); the cam detent adds closing torque F·(depth/ramp) fading linearly over the ramp. Ribbon stress σ=6M/(b·t²) — keep ≤150 ksi (Cr-Si). Ribbon length for the entered rate: ℓ=2πE·I/k, I=b·t³/12, E=30×10⁶ psi. Companion to docs/DESIGN.md — same math, same worked-example defaults.
